Get the latest version from the [PVcollib releases page](https://github.com/alekmaul/pvcollib/releases/latest).

Choose the archive for your system:

| System | Archive |
|---|---|
| Windows (64 bits) | `pvcollib_64b_windows.zip` |
| Linux (64 bits) | `pvcollib_64b_linux.zip` |
| macOS | `pvcollib_64b_darwin.zip` |

Then follow the [Installation & basic example](https://github.com/alekmaul/pvcollib/wiki/Installation-and-helloworld-example) page.
